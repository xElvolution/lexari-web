# Solana

Your agents use their own Solana wallets: check what they hold and send test USDC or SOL to an address you approve. Every transfer waits for your Confirm in chat.

## What agents can do

- Read its wallet address and balances
- Send test USDC or SOL on devnet, after you confirm
- Mainnet transfers (coming soon)

Chains: Solana. Website: https://solana.com. Docs: https://solana.com/docs.
