# Panta

Your agents browse Panta markets, read live YES / NO prices and get real quotes. On devnet a position you confirm is recorded as a paper position at Panta's real quote (Panta settles in mainnet USDC, so no money moves yet).

## What agents can do

- Browse markets and live YES / NO prices
- Real quotes for a position
- Paper positions on devnet, after you confirm
- Positions marked to live prices, closed with profit and loss
- Real positions and claims (mainnet) (coming soon)

Chains: Solana. Website: https://panta.market. Docs: https://docs.panta.market.
