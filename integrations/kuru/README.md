# Kuru

Your agents get Kuru routes on Monad testnet and swap from their own Monad wallet. Every swap waits for your Confirm inside your per-trade and slippage limits. Test tokens only.

## What agents can do

- Live routes and quotes on Monad testnet
- Swaps, after you confirm
- Mainnet (coming soon)

Chains: Monad testnet. Website: https://www.kuru.io. Docs: https://docs.kuru.io.
