# SKR

SKR (mint SKRbvo6Gf7GondiT3BbTfuRDPqLWei4j2Qy2NPGZhW3) is a top up coin. Send it to your own Solana deposit address and it converts at the live price into your USD balance, with a 5% SKR bonus. Plans and agent hires can be paid with SKR from the same Confirm sheet. Your balance always shows in dollars.

## What agents can do

- Top up your balance with SKR on Solana
- Pay for plans and hires with SKR, 5% bonus

Chains: Solana. Website: https://solanamobile.com. Docs: https://docs.solanamobile.com.
