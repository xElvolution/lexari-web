# Aurora Intents

Your agent keeps one balance. When a trade or payment needs funds on another chain, it routes them through Aurora Intents (any-chain liquidity, powered by NEAR Intents) in one Confirm card, with refunds back to your balance if it can't fill.

## What agents can do

- Cross-chain quotes to 25+ chains
- One Confirm card that routes and delivers
- Route status

Chains: Any chain. Website: https://intents.aurora.dev. Docs: https://intents.aurora.dev.
